Clan System — Full Update (Now Live for Testing)
Clans received a major overhaul. The update is still in progress, but everything listed below is already usable. Jump in and try it out.

Finding & Joining a Clan
•     Browse all clans with member count, average level, and recruiting status
•     View public clan profiles with stats, description, XP bar, and member list
•     Apply to open clans with an optional message
•     Accept or decline invitations from clan officers

Clan Dashboard
•     Fully integrated single‑page dashboard
•     Overview tab shows quick stats, recent activity, war alerts, and shortcuts
•     Tabs stay where you left them after every action

Roster
•     Full member list sorted by rank
•     Click any member to view their profile

Hit List
•     Post bounties on enemy players
•     Tracks how many clan members attacked each target

Activity Log
•     Chronological feed of all clan events: joins, kicks, promotions, wars, settings changes, and more

Shared Intel
•     Members can share spy reports and attack results
•     Intel tab shows recent shared reports with target info
•     Requires the “View Intel” permission

Clan Bank
•     Deposit up to 70% of your on‑hand gold (3 deposits/day)
•     50% goes to clan treasury, 50% becomes your personal secret stash
•     Withdraw up to 50% of your total deposits anytime
•     Banked gold is hidden from rankings, profiles, and spy ops
•     Quick-pick buttons for fast input
•     Full transaction history visible to all members
•     Top contributors shown on the dashboard

Stats
•     Aggregated clan-wide combat stats: attack wins, defense wins, gold stolen, fort damage, kills, spy ops
•     Leaderboards for top attackers, defenders, and richest members

Wars
•     Declare war using gold from the clan bank
•     Active war banner with live score and countdown
•     Accept or decline incoming war declarations
•     War kills tracked per member
•     Full war history with results

Recruitment
•     Invite players by name with an optional message
•     Review and accept/reject applications
•     Track pending invites with expiry timers
•     Toggle a Recruiting badge on your public clan profile

Member Management
•     Promote and demote members through rank hierarchy
•     Kick members with confirmation
•     Transfer leadership
•     Disband the clan or leave from the danger zone

Announcements
•     Send mass messages to all members or specific ranks
•     Preview recipient count before sending
•     Announcement history with read/unread tracking
•     Detailed view shows exactly who read each announcement

Settings
Profile: name, tag, description, internal message, icon upload, recruiting toggle
Ranks: create custom ranks with permissions (Invite, Kick, Manage, View Intel, Announce)
•     Edit or delete ranks
•     Set default rank for new members
•     Leader rank is permanent and full‑permission

Clan XP & Leveling
•     Clans earn XP from member activity
•     XP bar + level shown on dashboard and public profile
•     Visible to everyone visiting your clan page
